About Us

A small family-run hotel built around a garden.

Mother's Garden Boutique Hotel began as a family home with a large garden full of roses, herbs and fruit trees. Today it welcomes guests who want a quiet, personal stay away from the crowds.

Many of the herbs and vegetables you will taste in our cafe are grown just outside the door. Our team is happy to help with local tips, transfers and special occasions.
